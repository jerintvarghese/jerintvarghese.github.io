How I work

From question to screen.

01

Understand

Start with the people who'll use it. What are they trying to get done, and where does it hurt today?

02

Define

Turn findings into a clear problem, user flows and the few things the design must get right.

03

Prototype

Sketch, wireframe, then high-fidelity screens. Clickable early, so ideas get tested, not debated.

04

Test & ship

Put it in front of users, fix what trips them up, and hand off or build a front end that matches the design.
